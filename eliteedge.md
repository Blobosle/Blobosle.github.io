**Elite Edge** is my end‑to‑end college‑basketball analytics suite.

Each week it pulls fresh KenPom data (2013 – 2025), stores it in a unified PostgreSQL schema, and retrains two predictive models: one that ranks all 68 tournament teams and another that zeroes in on Final Four odds.

I surface the results through three dashboards. The flagship Final Four view spotlights key stats, recent form, season records, and even arena details for every favorite. A second dashboard maps the full bracket and adds a “Trapezoid of Excellence” chart to reveal sleepers at a glance. For deeper dives, conference‑themed pages—starting with a Big Ten palette—can be spun up with one config file.

Every Monday I publish a short blog post that recaps model performance, explains upsets, and updates probabilities. The dashboards are mobile‑friendly, theme‑aware, and ready to scale as new data sources land in the database.
